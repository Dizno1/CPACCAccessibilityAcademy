CPACC Preparation Course - Domain 4 and Domain 5 Redesign Notes

Purpose
This note records the revised instructional direction for Domains 4 and 5. Domains 1, 2, and 3 now use a richer instructional style with scenarios, professional perspective, common misconceptions, and CPACC exam focus. Domains 4 and 5 have been updated to match that style.

Domain 4 revised lesson plan
1. Assistive Technology Foundations
2. Visual Access Technologies
3. Communication and Interaction Technologies
4. Alternative Input, Cognitive Supports, and Emerging Technologies
5. Domain 4 Comprehensive Review

Domain 4 instructional purpose
Domain 4 should not read like a device inventory. It should teach how people use technology to access information, communicate, interact, and complete real tasks. The theme is user need plus assistive technology plus accessible design.

Domain 5 revised lesson plan
1. Accessibility Laws and Why They Exist
2. Major U.S. Accessibility Laws
3. Accessibility Standards
4. Organizational Accessibility
5. Domain 5 Comprehensive Review

Domain 5 instructional purpose
Domain 5 should not ask learners to memorize acronyms in isolation. It should teach the relationship between laws, standards, procurement, VPATs, accessibility statements, governance, training, remediation, and continuous improvement.
