Open Door Accessibility Academy V11.5 Test Build

This is a development build and is not a finished product.

For this testing cycle, please focus on Domain 1 and Domain 2 only. Domains 3 through 5 are included so testers can understand the full CPACC structure, but they are not ready for formal testing yet.

To start testing, open index.html in your preferred browser.

Recommended testing areas:

1. Move through Domain 1 and Domain 2 lessons.
2. Mark parts complete.
3. Mark lessons complete.
4. Try Reinforce Learning, Practice Questions, and Challenge Mode after completing a lesson.
5. Use the Academy Library from within a lesson and test Return To Lesson.
6. Type notes and use Insert Note Marker.
7. Review whether the content feels like a real course rather than a list of study notes.

Known scope note:

Domains 3 through 5 are planned for later builds. Please do not report missing depth in those domains as a defect for this testing cycle.
